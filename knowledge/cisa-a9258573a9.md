MZ Automation lib60870 Out-of-Bounds Read Vulnerabilities

CISA disclosed two out-of-bounds read vulnerabilities in MZ Automation lib60870 version 2.4.0 that could enable device denial of service through crafted IEC 60870-5-104 protocol frames. Both CVE-2026-61893 and CVE-2026-63033 carry CVSS scores of 6.5-6.9 and require network access without authentication. The vendor recommends upgrading to version 2.4.1 and has published advisories on GitHub.
