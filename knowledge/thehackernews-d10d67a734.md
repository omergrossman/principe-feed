CISA Adds Two Critical Citrix NetScaler Flaws to Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog

CISA has cataloged two critical vulnerabilities affecting Citrix NetScaler ADC and Gateway products following confirmation of active exploitation in the wild. The flaws, including CVE-2026-88771 (CVSS 9.5), involve improper input validation and allow unauthenticated attackers to compromise affected systems.
