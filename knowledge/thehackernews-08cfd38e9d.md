Attackers Compromised Danfoss Controllers to Disable Refrigeration in French Supermarkets

Hundreds of French supermarket locations experienced coordinated tampering with their refrigeration systems on October 11, with attackers gaining remote access through Danfoss industrial controllers used for temperature management. The attackers elevated temperatures to approximately 50°C, resulting in spoilage of stored food inventory across multiple retail chains. The compromise leveraged maintenance access channels typically used by authorized service providers.
