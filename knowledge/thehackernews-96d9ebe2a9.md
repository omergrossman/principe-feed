GoldenEyeDog Subgroup Linked to DigiCert Breach and Code-Signing Certificate Theft

Researchers have attributed the April 2026 DigiCert incident to CylindricalCanine, identified as a sub-group of GoldenEyeDog, a Chinese threat actor previously focused on gambling and gaming sectors. The breach resulted in theft of code-signing certificates, enabling potential supply-chain attack vectors. Technical analysis was provided by Expel.
