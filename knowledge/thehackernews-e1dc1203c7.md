FBI Attributes $6.43 Billion Binance Crypto Theft to North Korean Group TraderTraitor

U.S. law enforcement agencies have formally attributed a cryptocurrency theft exceeding $6 billion from Binance to TraderTraitor, a North Korean-aligned hacking group also known by multiple aliases including Jade Sleet and Pressure Chollima. The attribution was made jointly by the FBI, U.S. Secret Service, and IRS Criminal Investigation.
