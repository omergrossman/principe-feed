CISA Advisory: ANDRITZ HIPASE-250 and 250 SCALA Multiple Vulnerabilities

CISA published an advisory covering four vulnerabilities affecting ANDRITZ HIPASE-250 and 250 SCALA industrial control systems (versions ≤7.20). Flaws include reversible password storage, missing authentication on critical endpoints, unauthenticated logging control, and hard-coded VNC credentials on engineering workstations. ANDRITZ has released patches in versions 8.00.00 and 8.15.00.
