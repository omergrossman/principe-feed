Weintek cMT3092X Multiple Privilege Escalation and Authentication Vulnerabilities

CISA disclosed four vulnerabilities in Weintek cMT3092X HMI devices affecting firmware versions prior to 20210218 and EasyWeb prior to v2.1.20. These flaws enable non-privileged users to escalate privileges through cookie/token manipulation, access plaintext-stored passwords, and modify read-only data. Weintek has released patch cmt_typeB_20260316_007.patch containing EasyWeb 2.3.17-typeb as the remediation.
