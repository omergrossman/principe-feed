FortiBleed Campaign Targets 430,000+ FortiGate Firewalls in Credential Harvesting Operation

A Russian-speaking financially-motivated initial access broker has conducted a large-scale credential harvesting campaign against over 430,000 FortiGate firewalls worldwide since February 2026. The operation involves credential collection, service enumeration, brute-force attacks, and deployment of custom malware to establish initial access for downstream exploitation.
