Ransom Busters Group Offers Data Deletion Services to Ransomware Victims

A threat actor operating under the Ransom Busters moniker is contacting organizations affected by ransomware attacks, claiming to have gained access to threat actors' servers and offering to delete stolen data for fees between $20,000 and $60,000. The unsolicited outreach represents an unusual intermediary model within the ransomware ecosystem, positioning itself as a recovery service rather than a traditional extortion operation.
