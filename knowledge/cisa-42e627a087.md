Moxa AIG-302 ThingsPro Edge Vulnerabilities Patched

CISA disclosed two vulnerabilities in Moxa AIG-302 ThingsPro Edge industrial computing devices: a missing authorization flaw (CVSS 6.8–6.9) and a hard-coded cryptographic key vulnerability (CVSS 8.1–8.6). Both require adjacent network access and do not support remote exploitation. Moxa released patch v1.4.1 to remediate the issues.
