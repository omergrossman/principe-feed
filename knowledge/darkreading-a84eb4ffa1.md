OnlyFans Copyright Takedowns Reveal Compromised Government and University Websites

Attackers are leveraging compromised university and government websites to file fraudulent copyright takedown requests associated with OnlyFans content, exploiting these trusted domains to manipulate search rankings. Security researchers at UpGuard identified the pattern and note that vulnerable institutional websites are being weaponized in this scheme. Organizations need to strengthen access controls and monitor for unauthorized DMCA filing activity.
