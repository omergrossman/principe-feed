Armenia Detains Russian Tourist on U.S. REvil Extradition Request; Defense Claims Mistaken Identity

Armenian authorities detained a Russian national named Aleksandr Ermakov at Yerevan airport on June 28 following a U.S. extradition warrant alleging involvement in REvil ransomware operations. The detainee's legal representatives contend the arrest stems from a case of mistaken identity, with border officials reportedly matching him against a photo from social media.
