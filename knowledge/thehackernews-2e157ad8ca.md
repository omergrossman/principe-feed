BeyondTrust Patches Critical Authentication Bypass Vulnerabilities in Remote Support Products

BeyondTrust released security updates addressing two critical pre-authentication vulnerabilities in its Remote Support and Privileged Remote Access products. The flaws, including CVE-2026-40138 with a CVSS score of 9.2, could permit unauthenticated attackers to gain control of affected systems. Organizations using these products should apply the patches promptly.
