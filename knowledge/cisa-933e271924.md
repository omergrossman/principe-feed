CISA Adds Palo Alto Networks PAN-OS Authentication Bypass to Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog

CISA has added CVE-2026-0257, an authentication bypass vulnerability in Palo Alto Networks PAN-OS, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog following evidence of active exploitation. Federal agencies are required under BOD 22-01 to remediate the vulnerability by a specified deadline, while CISA recommends all organizations prioritize patching to reduce attack surface.
