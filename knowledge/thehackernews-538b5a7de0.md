Attacker Established Persistent Access Using Tailscale and OpenSSH After C2 Infrastructure Failure

An attacker who compromised a French automotive company installed legitimate remote access tools (OpenSSH and Tailscale) on victim systems as a fallback persistence mechanism independent of their command-and-control infrastructure. The tactic allowed continued unauthorized access even after the attacker's primary C2 server went offline, demonstrating operational resilience through abuse of standard administration tools.
