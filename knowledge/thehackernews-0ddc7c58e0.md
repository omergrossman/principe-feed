CISA Adds Five Active Exploitations to KEV Catalog

CISA has cataloged five vulnerabilities affecting JFrog Artifactory, ConnectWise ScreenConnect, and MikroTik RouterOS as known exploited vulnerabilities, based on confirmed active exploitation reports. The additions include a critical authorization flaw in Artifactory with a CVSS score of 8.1, among others impacting widely-deployed infrastructure and remote access solutions.
