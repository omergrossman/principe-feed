CISA Advisory: Authentication and Resource Control Weaknesses in CPDLC over ATN-B1 Protocol

Five vulnerabilities in the CPDLC over ATN-B1 data link communications protocol affect all versions worldwide. The flaws stem from unauthenticated radio frequency messaging and insufficient resource controls, enabling remote injection of false clearances, session disruption, and broadcast disconnections that could degrade air traffic operational margins. CISA reports no known active exploitation and notes the vulnerabilities require specific lab conditions and high attack complexity to trigger.
