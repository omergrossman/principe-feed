CISA Adds Two Zammad Vulnerabilities to Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog

CISA has catalogued two actively exploited vulnerabilities in Zammad software: a session fixation flaw (CVE-2026-102489) and an improper privilege management issue (CVE-2026-102490). Federal agencies are required under BOD 26-04 to prioritize patching KEV-listed vulnerabilities on exposed assets, with CISA encouraging all organizations to adopt similar risk-based remediation practices.
