Russian State-Backed Group Exploited Zimbra Zero-Day for Email and 2FA Theft

A Russian state-sponsored espionage group leveraged a previously unknown vulnerability in Zimbra's webmail platform to access victim mailboxes, targeting the past 90 days of email communications, directory listings, stored browser passwords, and two-factor authentication recovery codes. The exploit required only message opening to activate. U.S. government agencies including NSA and CISA have published guidance on the vulnerability.
