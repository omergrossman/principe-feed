Attackers Chain Ivanti Connect Secure Flaws for Unauthenticated Root Access

CISA added two vulnerabilities affecting Ivanti Connect Secure to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog following active exploitation. Attackers are chaining these flaws to achieve unauthenticated root access, with one vulnerability (CVE-2026-111357, CVSS 8.8) involving missing authentication controls on critical functions.
