Zyxel GS1900 Switch Vulnerability Added to CISA KEV Catalog Due to Active Exploitation

CISA has listed CVE-2026-7273, a stack-based buffer overflow in Zyxel GS1900 series switches (CVSS 8.8), in its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog following confirmed active exploitation. The flaw permits arbitrary code execution with elevated privileges and has been addressed in available patches.
