Siemens SIPLUS and SIMATIC Products Vulnerable to Copy Fail Vulnerability

Siemens has disclosed that multiple SIPLUS and SIMATIC product lines are affected by the Copy Fail vulnerability (CVE-2026-31431). Updates are available for several affected products, with additional fixes in preparation and interim countermeasures recommended where patches are unavailable. The vulnerability impacts SIMATIC AX Runtime environments, CN 4100 controllers, and a broad range of HMI panel models across multiple form factors and configurations.
