TeamPCP Supply Chain Campaign Extends to U.S. Government; Mini Shai-Hulud Framework Adoption Widens

The TeamPCP supply chain campaign has expanded to target U.S. government entities, with formal attribution now confirmed by government agencies. Following the open-sourcing of the Mini Shai-Hulud framework last month, broader threat actor adoption of the tool has accelerated the campaign's scope and impact across multiple victim categories.
