CISA and FBI Alert Election Officials to Targeted Cyber Threats Ahead of 2026 Midterms

CISA and the FBI issued guidance warning election and campaign officials of heightened cyber threat activity targeting the 2026 U.S. midterm elections. Threat actors employ phishing, smishing, and sophisticated social engineering techniques to compromise credentials and deploy malware, with particular interest in voter registration systems and campaign infrastructure. Both agencies recommend election officials implement standard security hygiene practices and maintain heightened awareness of suspicious communications.
