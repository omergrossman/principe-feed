2026 Breach Impact Study

Verizon and CyberAcuView released the inaugural Breach Impact Study, analyzing approximately 70,000 cyber insurance claims (38,000 with recorded payouts) covering insurable incidents from January 2019 through October 2025 in the United States. The study addresses a frequently requested question about financial impact quantification by examining ground-up losses—combining deductibles and incurred losses—across multiple loss types including ransomware and business email compromise claims. The dataset represents insurable loss only, functioning as a conservative lower bound of actual economic impact, as it excludes uninsured losses, reputational damage, and non-claim costs, with additional limitations from policy sublimits and insurance tower structures. The report segments findings by incident type, industry sector (including healthcare, manufacturing, retail, and small/medium-sized businesses), and organizational size, utilizing statistical rigor and uncertainty visualization consistent with the Data Breach Investigations Report methodology.
