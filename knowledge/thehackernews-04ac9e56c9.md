ShinyHunters Exploits Oracle PeopleSoft Zero-Day to Breach Universities

ShinyHunters (tracked as UNC6240 by Mandiant) exploited an unpatched Oracle PeopleSoft vulnerability to compromise enterprise systems and extract data, with universities as primary targets. The threat actor conducted extortion campaigns between late May and early June 2026. Oracle's advisory was published after the active exploitation window, indicating a zero-day exposure period.
