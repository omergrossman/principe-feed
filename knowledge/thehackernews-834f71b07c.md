Claude AI Demonstrates Key Recovery Attack Against HAWK-256 Post-Quantum Scheme

Anthropic's Claude model assisted in deriving an end-to-end key-recovery attack against the HAWK-256 post-quantum signature scheme by exploiting an unused lattice symmetry. The research also identified significant performance improvements for attacking seven-round AES-128, achieving 200-800x speedup. The HAWK attack requires approximately 3.7 hours on a 96-core server for end-to-end key recovery.
