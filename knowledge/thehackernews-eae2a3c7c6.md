Australian Authorities Disrupt Amadey Malware Infrastructure, Isolating 180,000 Infected Hosts

The Australian Signals Directorate successfully gained access to the backend infrastructure of the Amadey malware operation and severed command-and-control communications with approximately 180,000 compromised devices globally. The disruption prevents operators from exfiltrating data or executing further commands on affected systems. This action represents a coordinated takedown of a widely distributed botnet affecting multiple jurisdictions.
