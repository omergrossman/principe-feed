AV-Comparatives Releases 2025 Endpoint Prevention & Response Test Results

AV-Comparatives published its 2025 EPR test results, evaluating enterprise endpoint solutions against 50 advanced attack scenarios mapped to MITRE ATT&CK, with assessment spanning prevention, detection, response, and financial impact. Ten vendors achieved EPR Certification 2025, including Bitdefender, CrowdStrike, Palo Alto Networks, and others, while two failed certification. The evaluation combines technical effectiveness with operational and cost-efficiency modeling to provide enterprises with comparative benchmarking for endpoint security selection.
