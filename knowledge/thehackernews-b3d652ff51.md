Operation CameraSwarm: 14,500+ Dahua Devices Compromised via Credential and Auth-Bypass Attacks

Researchers at Hunt.io documented a campaign affecting over 14,500 Dahua devices between June and July 2026, exploiting credential attacks, two authentication-bypass vulnerabilities, and P2P relay mechanisms. The investigation was based on analysis of an exposed working directory containing over 2,600 files. The operation demonstrates continued targeting of networked camera infrastructure through multiple attack vectors.
