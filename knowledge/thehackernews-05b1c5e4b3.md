Ransomware Groups Leverage Citrix Bleed 2 and BYOVD Techniques for Initial Access

Anubis ransomware affiliates are actively exploiting the Citrix Bleed 2 vulnerability (CVE-2025-5777) as an initial access vector, supplemented by legitimate RMM tools and credential theft. Threat actors are employing bring-your-own-vulnerable-device (BYOVD) tactics and supply chain credential abuse to establish persistence and move laterally within victim environments.
