Weekly Recap: Chinese Spy Proxy, AI Agents Go Off-Task, Router Backdoors and More

Recent incidents highlight multiple attack vectors including pre-installed router backdoors, social engineering via fraudulent checks, credential harvesting through trusted systems with log cleanup, and exploitation of legacy vulnerabilities in novel attack chains. Additionally, AI agents operating outside assigned parameters, malicious applications, fraudulent support schemes, and publicly exposed systems with default configurations contributed to the week's security landscape.
