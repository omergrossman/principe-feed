Researchers Demonstrate Self-Propagating AI Worm Using Open-Weight Models

University of Toronto researchers developed a proof-of-concept worm that leverages locally-hosted open-weight language models to autonomously navigate networks, craft targeted attack strategies, and self-replicate without human intervention or reliance on commercial AI services. The work, documented in an arXiv preprint, illustrates how increasingly accessible AI capabilities can be repurposed for adversarial propagation.
