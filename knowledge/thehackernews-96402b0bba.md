Cisco SD-WAN Vulnerability Exploited to Deploy Encryption Key-Stealing Malware

Attackers leveraged a critical Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN vulnerability (patched in February) to deploy custom malware termed KeyDrift on SD-WAN control systems. The malware was designed to extract and store encryption keys used to protect inter-site traffic. Cisco Talos documented these incidents as historical in nature, though specific timing remains undisclosed.
