Hitachi Energy REB500 Vulnerabilities in libexpat Library

Hitachi Energy has disclosed two vulnerabilities affecting REB500 versions 8.3.3.1 and earlier, both stemming from the libexpat library used in IEC 61850 functionality. The flaws—uncontrolled recursion and unthrottled resource allocation—can be exploited by authenticated users to trigger denial of service conditions or potential memory corruption. Mitigation requires updating to version 8.3.4.0.
