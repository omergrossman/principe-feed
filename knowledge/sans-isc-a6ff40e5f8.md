Microsoft September 2026 Patch Tuesday: 973 Vulnerabilities Addressed

Microsoft released patches for 973 vulnerabilities in its September 2026 Patch Tuesday cycle, setting a new monthly record and exceeding the previous high of 664 from July 2026. The release included 113 critical-severity fixes, with two vulnerabilities confirmed as actively exploited in the wild. Significant patches addressed privilege escalation in Windows and remote code execution flaws in Skype for Business, MSMQ, and RRAS.
