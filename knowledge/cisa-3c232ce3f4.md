CISA Adds Google Pixel Authorization Flaw to Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog

CISA has cataloged CVE-2026-58704, an improper authorization vulnerability in Google Pixel devices, after confirming active exploitation in the wild. The addition reinforces federal vulnerability management obligations under BOD 26-04, which requires U.S. civilian agencies to prioritize patching high-risk KEV-listed vulnerabilities on exposed systems. CISA recommends all organizations adopt similar risk-based remediation practices for vulnerabilities in the KEV Catalog.
