AI-Generated Content Degrading Attack Quality, Legitimate Threat Actors Remain Selective

Research indicates that AI-generated content is reducing the sophistication of cyberattacks, with threat actors of significant capability showing limited adoption of AI tools. The proliferation of low-quality AI-assisted attacks suggests a bifurcation in the threat landscape, where mass-market attack campaigns suffer from reduced efficacy while established adversaries continue relying on human expertise and traditional methods.
