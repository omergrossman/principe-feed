CISA Advisory: Three High-Severity Vulnerabilities in Labcenter Proteus 9

CISA disclosed three memory-corruption vulnerabilities in Labcenter Proteus 9 (version 9.1_SP4_Build_42914): an out-of-bounds write, stack-based buffer overflow, and use-after-free flaw. All three flaws carry CVSS scores of 7.8–8.4 and can permit arbitrary code execution when specially crafted files are processed. Labcenter Electronics recommends updating to version 9.2 SP0 to remediate the issues.
