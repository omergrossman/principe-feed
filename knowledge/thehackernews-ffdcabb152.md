AOMEI Backupper Update Servers Compromised to Distribute Backdoor

Attackers gained access to AOMEI Backupper's update infrastructure and repeatedly modified files over brief windows between May and July 2026, allowing them to inject a backdoor into the Windows backup utility when users checked for updates during those periods. The compromise was detected through coordinated analysis by Google Threat Intelligence Group, Mandiant, and ManageEngine's threat team.
