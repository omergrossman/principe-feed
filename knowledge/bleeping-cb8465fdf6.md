FTC Reports Record Imposter Scam Losses Reaching $3.5 Billion in 2025

The Federal Trade Commission documented $3.5 billion in losses attributed to imposter scams during 2025, reflecting a significant upward trend in fraud severity over the past five years. Reported losses have nearly tripled since 2020, indicating an expanding attack surface and evolving social engineering tactics that continue to impact U.S. consumers.
