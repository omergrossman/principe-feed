Hitachi Energy MicroSCADA X SYS600 Multiple Vulnerabilities

CISA disclosed four vulnerabilities affecting Hitachi Energy MicroSCADA X SYS600 versions 10.7 and prior, including CSV injection, authentication bypass, and privilege escalation flaws with CVSS scores ranging from 5.0 to 8.5. Affected deployments span critical infrastructure globally; vendor remediation involves upgrading to version 10.8 with additional mitigation strategies documented in administrative guides.
