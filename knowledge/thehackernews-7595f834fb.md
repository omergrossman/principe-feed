NetScaler ADC/Gateway Flaw Exploited for Root Access with WHIPSHOT/SLAPSHOT Malware

Threat actors have exploited a patched vulnerability in Citrix NetScaler ADC and Gateway appliances to achieve root-level access across multiple sectors in North America and Europe. The campaign, documented by Mandiant and Google Threat Intelligence, deployed custom malware variants against government, financial services, technology, education, and legal organizations.
