NadMesh Botnet Targets Exposed AI Services for Cloud Credentials

A Go-based botnet designated NadMesh has been actively scanning for exposed AI infrastructure including ComfyUI, Ollama, and n8n instances since early July, harvesting AWS keys and Kubernetes tokens from inadequately secured deployments. The operator's dashboard indicates possession of over 3,800 unique AWS credentials, suggesting widespread compromise of AI services that are commonly deployed with minimal network isolation.
