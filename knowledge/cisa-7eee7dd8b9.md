CISA Adds Mirasvit Cache Plugin Deserialization Flaw to Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog

CISA has cataloged CVE-2026-45247, a deserialization vulnerability in Mirasvit Full Page Cache Warmer, following confirmed evidence of active exploitation. The vulnerability poses material risk to federal systems and triggers mandatory remediation requirements under BOD 22-01 for civilian executive branch agencies. CISA recommends all organizations prioritize patching this and other KEV-listed flaws as part of standard vulnerability management.
