WhatsApp VBScript Campaign Distributes ManageEngine RMM Tool via Malicious Documents

A multi-region campaign is delivering malicious VBScript files through WhatsApp direct messages, disguised as legitimate documents, to facilitate unauthorized installation of ManageEngine RMM software. The threat targets WhatsApp Desktop and Web users across multiple countries including Malaysia, Brazil, India, Mexico, Singapore, the UK, Spain, Taiwan, and Australia. Kaspersky researchers identified the campaign as actively distributing the payload to gain remote access capabilities on compromised systems.
