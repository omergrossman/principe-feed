BlackX UEFI Bootkit Targets Central Asian Diplomatic Entities

A newly identified UEFI bootkit named BlackX has been deployed against diplomatic organizations in Tajikistan, Uzbekistan, and Turkmenistan, exploiting gaps in Secure Boot revocation mechanisms on patched Windows systems. Kaspersky attributes the campaign to APT41, a state-sponsored group with suspected Chinese nexus, indicating a sophisticated supply-chain or firmware-level attack capability.
