Three Threat Groups Target Russian Enterprises With Backdoors, Ransomware, and Wipers

Kaspersky has identified coordinated activity from three threat clusters—NightEagle, Hacking Cat, and Toy Ghouls—targeting Russian enterprises with backdoors, ransomware, and destructive capabilities. NightEagle, active since at least 2023, has deployed new persistence and lateral movement techniques in recent campaigns.
