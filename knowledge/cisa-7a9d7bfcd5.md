AutomationDirect Productivity Suite Multiple Memory Corruption Vulnerabilities

CISA disclosed six vulnerabilities in AutomationDirect Productivity Suite versions up to v4.6.2.2, involving out-of-bounds read/write and divide-by-zero conditions requiring local or physical access. These flaws could enable privilege escalation, information disclosure, or denial of service in critical manufacturing environments. The vendor recommends updating to v4.7.0.47 or above, with network isolation and access controls recommended as interim mitigations.
