Antino Backdoor Targets Asian Government Organizations via Outlook and OneDrive

A China-linked threat actor has deployed a previously undocumented backdoor named Antino against government and policy organizations across seven Asian countries, including Taiwan, India, and the Philippines. The backdoor leverages Outlook and OneDrive as command-and-control channels, exploiting legitimate cloud services to evade detection. Cisco Talos is tracking the campaign and the associated threat cluster.
