Administrative Rights Governance in Entra ID

Organizations should regularly audit administrative privileges in Entra ID to identify excessive permissions, including former employees retaining access and overly-privileged support staff. Misaligned role-based access control—such as entry-level support staff with global admin or Intune policy modification rights—creates unnecessary risk and is a common audit finding. Systematic review of administrative accounts aligns with CIS Controls v8 guidance on access control management.
