Schneider Electric EasyLogic T150 and Saitel DP RTU Credential Exposure Vulnerabilities

CISA disclosed two vulnerabilities in Schneider Electric's EasyLogic T150 and Saitel DP remote terminal units affecting critical infrastructure. CVE-2026-9650 and CVE-2026-9651 allow unauthenticated or privileged local attackers to access stored credentials and password hashes with CVSS scores of 7.5 and 8.7 respectively. Schneider Electric has released firmware patches (versions 11.06.32 and 11.06.38) available through their Customer Care Center.
