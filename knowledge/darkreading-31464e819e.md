Linux Botnet Evooo1Bot Extends Mirai-Based Functionality Beyond DDoS Operations

Evooo1Bot, a Linux botnet derived from Mirai source code, has evolved beyond traditional distributed denial-of-service attacks to incorporate exploitation capabilities, credential harvesting, and reverse SOCKS proxy functionality. These additions enable attackers to leverage compromised devices as persistent infrastructure for lateral movement and sustained access rather than ephemeral attack vectors.
