HalluSquatting Attack Exploits AI Coding Assistants' Hallucinations to Deliver Malware

Researchers have identified a new attack method whereby threat actors exploit AI coding assistants' tendency to generate nonexistent package names. By registering domains or repositories matching these fabricated names before legitimate packages exist, attackers can intercept requests from AI tools and deliver botnet malware to developers. The technique leverages the gap between AI hallucination and real-world package ecosystems.
