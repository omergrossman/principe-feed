Cisco ASA and FTD Remote Denial-of-Service Vulnerability Under Active Exploitation

Cisco has disclosed CVE-2026-20349, a high-severity flaw in Secure Firewall ASA and FTD software that stems from inadequate validation of HTTP request handling. The vulnerability, carrying a CVSS score of 8.6, permits unauthenticated remote attackers to trigger denial-of-service conditions and is currently being exploited in the wild.
