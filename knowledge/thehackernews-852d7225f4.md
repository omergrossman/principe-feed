Fake Notepad++ Plugin Distributes MATCHBOIL.V2 Malware in UAC-0099 Campaign

CERT-UA identified a campaign by Russia-aligned threat cluster UAC-0099 distributing MATCHBOIL.V2 malware through counterfeit Notepad++ plugins. The trojanized plugins target Windows systems and represent an evolution of the group's previous tactics, which included exploitation of WinRAR vulnerabilities.
