Critical SSRF Vulnerability in SonicWall SMA 1000 Under Active Exploitation

A maximum-severity server-side request forgery (SSRF) flaw in SonicWall SMA 1000 appliances is being actively exploited in the wild. The vulnerability exposes organizations running affected devices to unauthorized access and lateral movement risks. SonicWall customers are advised to apply available patches or implement mitigations promptly.
