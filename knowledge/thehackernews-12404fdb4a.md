Threat actors exploit OpenSSH SSHole vulnerability shortly after disclosure

Researchers observed active exploitation attempts against CVE-2024-6386 (SSHole), a flaw in OpenSSH that permits unauthorized login to accounts without passwords configured. The vulnerability, rated 8.1 CVSS, affects Linux systems running OpenSSH on musl C library with PAM disabled; attackers achieved root-level access on monitoring infrastructure within hours of public disclosure.
