Five Eyes Cyber Security Agencies Statement on AI and Cyber Risk

The Five Eyes cyber security agencies (US, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand) issued a coordinated statement emphasizing that frontier AI models will fundamentally alter both offensive and defensive cyber capabilities within months rather than years. The agencies stress that cyber resilience is now a core business and leadership responsibility, not purely a technical function, requiring boards and executives to ensure controls perform under real incident conditions. Key recommended actions include implementing secure-by-design and secure-by-default practices, reducing attack surface, accelerating patching cycles to counter shortened vulnerability-to-exploitation windows, addressing legacy systems, strengthening identity and access controls, and preparing incident response plans. The statement calls on organizations to deliberately integrate AI tools into security operations to detect vulnerabilities earlier, monitor anomalies, and improve incident response speed while acknowledging that adversaries are already leveraging AI for faster, more effective attacks.
