CISA Adds PTC Windchill RCE Vulnerability to Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog

CISA added a critical remote code execution vulnerability affecting PTC Windchill PDMlink and PTC FlexPLM to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, based on evidence of active exploitation in the wild. The flaw impacts enterprise product data management and lifecycle management systems, with web shell deployment observed as part of ongoing attack campaigns.
