Silver Fox Group Deploys MODBEACON RAT with gRPC-Based C2 Communications

A China-linked cybercrime group designated Silver Fox has deployed MODBEACON, a Rust-based remote access trojan that uses gRPC streaming for encrypted command-and-control traffic. The group distributes the malware through counterfeit software installers leveraged via SEO poisoning, masking a more organized operation than initial indicators suggest.
