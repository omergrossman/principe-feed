INTERPOL Operation Dismantles Sniper Dz Phishing-as-a-Service Platform

An INTERPOL-coordinated operation spanning October 2025 to February 2026 disrupted Sniper Dz, a phishing-as-a-service platform active for over a decade. The effort, designated Operation Ramz, involved law enforcement from 13 Middle Eastern and North African nations and resulted in 201 arrests, including the platform's primary administrator.
