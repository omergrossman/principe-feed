GMO Internet Group Establishes $1M Bounty Program for FX Trading Cloud Breach Perpetrators

Japan's GMO Internet Group launched a reward program offering up to $1 million for information leading to the arrest or international wanted notice of attackers who compromised its Onamae.com Desktop Cloud service. The initiative follows the company's refusal to meet an extortion demand related to the breach affecting Windows desktops used for foreign exchange trading.
