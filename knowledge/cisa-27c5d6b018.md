Siemens Industrial Edge Management Authentication Bypass Vulnerability

A critical authentication bypass vulnerability in Siemens Industrial Edge Management allows unauthenticated attackers to reset user credentials and perform account takeovers by circumventing email verification. The flaw affects Industrial Edge Management Cloud (all versions), Pro V1 (versions 1.14.9, 2.2.0, 2.6.0), and other variants. Siemens has released patched versions (V1.15.20, V2.2.2, V2.9.1) and recommends immediate updates; affected organizations can temporarily disable the password reset function as a mitigation.
