FBI: Breaking Affiliate Trust Sped Along LockBit's Takedown

Operation Cronos, a multinational law-enforcement initiative, successfully disrupted LockBit, the largest ransomware group at the time, by exploiting fractures in trust relationships between the group's leadership and its affiliate network. The FBI's strategy leveraged these organizational weaknesses to accelerate the operation's effectiveness. The takedown demonstrates how targeting the operational cohesion of criminal enterprises can be as impactful as targeting technical infrastructure.
