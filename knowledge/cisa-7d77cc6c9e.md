Apollo Pharmacy Blood Glucose Monitoring System APG-01 BT Vulnerabilities

CISA disclosed two vulnerabilities in Apollo Pharmacy's APG-01 BT blood glucose monitor affecting firmware version 0x0110_v1.1.0. An attacker within Bluetooth range could passively intercept sensitive health data or disrupt device connectivity by monopolizing the BLE connection slot. The vendor did not respond to CISA coordination requests; exploitation requires proximity and no active attacks have been reported.
