Mirage2FA Campaign Targets 4,500 Organizations Across US and EU via Microsoft 365 Compromise

A phishing-as-a-service toolkit called Mirage2FA has targeted approximately 4,500 organizations between 2024 and 2026 by exploiting Microsoft 365 authentication flows to circumvent two-factor authentication mechanisms. Analysis indicates that roughly 48% of targeted email addresses experienced potential compromise. The campaign predominantly affected US-based enterprises, with secondary impacts in the EU.
