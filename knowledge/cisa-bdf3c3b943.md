CISA Adds Gitea Code Injection Vulnerability to Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog

CISA added CVE-2026-60004, a code injection flaw in Gitea, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog following evidence of active exploitation in the wild. The addition reinforces Binding Operational Directive 26-04, which mandates federal agencies prioritize patching KEV-listed vulnerabilities on internet-facing systems and investigate potential prior compromise. CISA encourages all organizations to adopt similar risk-based vulnerability management practices regardless of regulatory requirement.
