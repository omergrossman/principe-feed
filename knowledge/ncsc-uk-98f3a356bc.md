UK and International Partners Expose Russian State-Backed LAUNDRY BEAR Phishing Campaign

The UK's National Cyber Security Centre (GCHQ) and international partners have publicly identified a Russian state-supported cyber group designated LAUNDRY BEAR conducting targeted phishing operations against Western organisations. The campaign employs zero-click attack vectors, requiring no user interaction for compromise. The exposure aims to disrupt ongoing operations and raise awareness among potentially affected entities.
