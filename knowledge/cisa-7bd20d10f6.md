Critical Authentication Bypass and Credential Storage Flaws in Tycon Systems TPDIN-Monitor-WEB2

CISA disclosed two vulnerabilities in Tycon Systems TPDIN-Monitor-WEB2 version 2.3.9 affecting critical infrastructure environments. An authentication bypass flaw (CVSS 9.8) allows unauthenticated attackers to submit empty credentials to gain administrative access, potentially disrupting infrastructure or causing physical equipment damage. A second vulnerability exposes stored credentials in cleartext on the configuration page, enabling further network compromise.
