ACR Stealer Infostealer Exploits ClickFix Social Engineering to Exfiltrate Credentials and Enterprise Data

ACR Stealer, an infostealer active since 2024, leverages ClickFix social engineering tactics to trick users into executing commands via the Windows Run dialog, enabling theft of browser credentials, session tokens, and enterprise files from Microsoft 365, OneDrive, and SharePoint. Microsoft's Defender Experts team documented multiple delivery chains used in active campaigns targeting enterprise environments.
