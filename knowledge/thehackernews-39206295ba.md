Weekly Recap: Linux Kernel Flaws, AI Malware Tricks, Turla Backdoor, Infostealers

The past week highlighted multiple security concerns including a Linux kernel vulnerability (DirtyClone) enabling local privilege escalation, emerging AI-assisted malware techniques, and continued activity from the Turla backdoor and infostealer campaigns. The incidents underscore that attackers often exploit unpatched systems and legacy access paths rather than relying solely on sophisticated attack methods.
