GitHub Reduces Public Bug Bounty Rewards, Establishes Tiered Payout Structure

GitHub will implement significant reductions to its public bug bounty program effective July 27, 2026, cutting payouts by at least 50% across all severity levels, with critical vulnerabilities capped at $10,000 versus the previous $20,000-$30,000+ range. The platform is establishing a VIP invite-only tier offering $30,000 or higher for select researchers. Reports submitted before the effective date will maintain existing payout levels.
