AV-Comparatives EDR Detection Validation 2026 Results

AV-Comparatives published results from its 2026 EDR detection validation test, evaluating seven enterprise cybersecurity solutions against advanced threat scenarios comprising 14 attack steps plus signal-to-noise assessment. Nine products achieved certification under the independent evaluation methodology, including solutions from Bitdefender, ESET, Fortinet, G Data, Genian, Kaspersky, ManageEngine, Palo Alto Networks, and Sangfor.
