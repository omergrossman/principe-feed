Canadian Cybercriminal Pleads Guilty in Snowflake Extortion Campaign

A 26-year-old Canadian threat actor has pleaded guilty to orchestrating a computer fraud and extortion scheme targeting over 165 organizations using Snowflake's cloud platform. The individual also admitted to unauthorized access to telecommunications data affecting more than 100 million AT&T customers, marking a significant resolution in one of 2024's notable cybercrime cases.
