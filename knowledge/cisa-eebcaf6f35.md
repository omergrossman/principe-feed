Multiple Critical Vulnerabilities in Mira Hormone Monitor and Android App

CISA disclosed eight vulnerabilities affecting Mira Hormone Monitor firmware 1.7.1.47 and Android app 4.5.15.4, with CVSS scores ranging from 6.5 to 9.8. Flaws include missing authentication on critical functions, hardcoded credentials, and BLE spoofing that could allow unauthorized access to reproductive health data, device rebinding, and account takeover. Updates are available via app versions iOS 3.5.18 and Android 4.5.18, with firmware 01.07.01.53 deployed automatically upon connection.
