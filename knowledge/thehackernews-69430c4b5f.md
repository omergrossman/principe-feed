Malicious Twitch Browser Extension Compromised 31,000 User OAuth Tokens

A cross-platform browser extension marketed as a Twitch enhancement tool exfiltrated OAuth credentials from approximately 31,000 users to infrastructure associated with a Russian commercial bot service. The extension, distributed across Chrome and Firefox stores under the name 'Twitch Enhanced Viewer | JeetBot,' has been identified as malicious and likely removed from official marketplaces.
