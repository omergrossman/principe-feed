China-Linked Hackers Leverage ARTEX AI Framework in ConnectWise Supply Chain Compromise

Microsoft attributed a ConnectWise Cloud Backup supply chain compromise to Storm-0940, a China state-aligned threat actor. The attacker deployed ARTEX, a Go-based AI agent framework designed for penetration testing, to facilitate the attack. ARTEX was subsequently closed-sourced following its use in previous campaigns targeting South Korean financial institutions.
