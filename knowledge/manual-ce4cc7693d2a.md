Why Post-Quantum Cryptography Starts With Credentials

Organizations face a material risk from Harvest Now, Decrypt Later attacks, where adversaries capture encrypted data today for decryption once quantum computers mature—plausibly within 15 years according to Global Risk Institute surveys. The NSA's Commercial National Security Algorithm Suite 2.0 mandates quantum-resistant support by January 1, 2027, with full migration targets by 2035; NIST's draft IR 8547 similarly deprecates RSA-2048 and ECC P-256 after 2030. Credentials and non-human identities (service accounts, API keys) present concentrated risk due to their multi-year confidentiality lifetimes and poor inventory visibility. A credentials-first migration strategy should prioritize long-lived secrets by confidentiality duration and attack surface, adopt hybrid cryptography combining classical and quantum-resistant algorithms, and build crypto-agility into centralized credential systems to enable future algorithm updates without re-engineering.
