Russia-Aligned UAC-0099 Deploys GuardBreaker Technique to Evade AI-Assisted Malware Analysis

ESET researchers identified GuardBreaker, a technique employed by Russia-aligned threat actor UAC-0099 against Ukrainian targets, designed to circumvent LLM safety mechanisms and obstruct AI-assisted malware analysis. The method embeds adversarial prompts within malware payloads to disrupt automated detection and analysis workflows.
