Mitsubishi Electric Multiple FA Products Denial-of-Service Vulnerability (CVE-2025-3511)

CISA has published an update regarding CVE-2025-3511, affecting multiple Mitsubishi Electric factory automation products including CC-Link IE TSN modules, MELSEC controllers, and related networking components. A remote attacker can trigger denial-of-service conditions, timeouts, or communication delays by transmitting specially crafted UDP packets to vulnerable devices. The vulnerability stems from improper input validation in the Ethernet function and carries a CVSS score of 7.5.
