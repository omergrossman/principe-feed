Factory Implants Discovered in ZBT Router Firmware Enable Unauthenticated Root Access

VulnCheck disclosed two undocumented firmware implants in routers manufactured by Shenzhen Zhibotong Electronics (ZBT), tracked as CVE-2026-74232 and CVE-2026-74233. Both implants allow unauthenticated remote attackers to execute commands with root privileges on affected devices. The findings suggest deliberate insertion during manufacturing rather than post-release exploitation vectors.
