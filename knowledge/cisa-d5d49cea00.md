ABB T-MAC Plus Multiple Vulnerabilities (CVE-2025-14771 through CVE-2025-14774)

ABB disclosed four vulnerabilities in T-MAC Plus 4.0-24 affecting critical manufacturing infrastructure globally. The flaws include file disclosure, privilege escalation, stored XSS, and denial-of-service capabilities, with CVSS scores ranging from 8.0 to 9.9. Patch version 4.0-25 is available and ABB recommends immediate deployment.
