SharePoint and MikroTik RouterOS Vulnerabilities Added to CISA KEV Catalog

CISA has designated two vulnerabilities as actively exploited: a code injection flaw in Microsoft Office SharePoint (CVE-2026-65660, CVSS 8.8) and an unspecified RouterOS vulnerability. Both flaws are being leveraged in confirmed attacks and now appear on the agency's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, indicating organizations should prioritize remediation.
