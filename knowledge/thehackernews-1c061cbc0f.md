Lurking Lizard Operates Residential Proxy Botnet via Fake 7-Zip Installers

Researchers identified a threat actor named Lurking Lizard running a residential proxy service since at least August 2022, leveraging over 230 counterfeit domains and malicious 7-Zip installers to compromise devices. The infrastructure converts infected systems into proxy nodes for illicit traffic routing and data exfiltration purposes.
