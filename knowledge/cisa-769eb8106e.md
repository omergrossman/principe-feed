CISA Advisory: Critical Vulnerabilities in Guangzhou Juan IoT EseeCloud IP Camera Platform

CISA disclosed three vulnerabilities affecting EseeCloud App versions ≤4.9.6 and Firmware ≤4.9.86.0, including hard-coded credentials enabling unauthorized device access (CVSS 9.8), improper authorization allowing device rebinding to attacker accounts, and enumeration flaws permitting large-scale device discovery. The vendor did not engage with CISA's coordination process; affected organizations should contact the vendor directly or consider alternative solutions.
