CISO's Guide to Agentic AI

Anthropic's Deputy CISO Jason Clinton outlines a risk assessment framework for securing agentic AI deployments, centered on making agentic risk legible and bounded rather than pursuing zero risk. The framework uses four evaluation questions: what untrusted content does the agent ingest, what actions can it take and under whose identity, what is the blast radius of misalignment, and what observability exists for agent actions. A key concern is that misaligned agents operate at speeds (minutes to hours) incompatible with traditional insider-threat response timelines (Ponemon reports 67-day average containment); Clinton positions agentic systems on an identity spectrum from least-privilege system service accounts to human credentials, identifying the ambiguous middle ground as accountability gaps. Primary internal risks include data leakage via insufficiently governed cross-system connections and prompt injection attacks, though model robustness against injection is improving. Anthropic's default deployment posture is admin-paced rollout with telemetry monitoring before access expansion.
