CISA Advisory: Multiple Vulnerabilities in J4Care dcm4che DICOM Archive

CISA identified six vulnerabilities in J4Care dcm4chee-arc-light 5.35.1 affecting medical imaging systems worldwide. Flaws include missing authorization controls enabling medical image deletion and patient record manipulation, parsing logic defects causing denial-of-service, and default authentication bypass permitting unauthorized DICOM data access. J4Care has released version 5.35.2 with fixes.
