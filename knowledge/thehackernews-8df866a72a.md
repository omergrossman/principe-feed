U.S. Disrupts Xinbi Guarantee Scam Marketplace, Seizes $52.8 Million in Crypto

The U.S. Department of Justice conducted coordinated enforcement operations against Xinbi Guarantee, an illicit marketplace facilitating online scams. Actions included shuttering Telegram channels, confiscating two cryptocurrency wallets totaling $52.8 million, and deploying personnel to Madagascar to dismantle 13 scam compounds operated by Chinese organized crime networks.
