CISA Adds SolarWinds Serv-U Vulnerability to Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog

CISA has cataloged CVE-2026-28318, a denial-of-service vulnerability in SolarWinds Serv-U, as actively exploited. The vulnerability joins CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog, which tracks CVEs posing significant risk to federal systems. CISA recommends all organizations prioritize remediation of catalog vulnerabilities as part of standard vulnerability management practices.
