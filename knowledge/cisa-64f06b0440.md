CISA Adds SolarWinds Serv-U Vulnerability to Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog

CISA has cataloged CVE-2026-28318, a denial-of-service vulnerability in SolarWinds Serv-U, as an actively exploited vulnerability. The addition reinforces BOD 22-01 requirements for federal agencies to remediate known exploited vulnerabilities by established deadlines. CISA recommends all organizations prioritize patching KEV Catalog vulnerabilities as part of standard vulnerability management.
