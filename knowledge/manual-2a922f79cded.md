The Great CNAPP Re-bundle

Major cloud security vendors are restructuring their CNAPP portfolios away from integrated posture platforms toward specialized solutions. Check Point is transitioning CloudGuard to CloudGuard WAF and partnering with Wiz for posture capabilities, while Palo Alto is shifting focus from Prisma Cloud to Cortex Cloud—a runtime-centric data lake for SOC teams. The analyst argues that posture scanning and asset vulnerability work have consolidated around Wiz and competitors like Aqua, Orca, and Sysdig, prompting larger vendors to pursue adjacent markets including AI security, runtime detection/response (CDR/CADR), continuous threat exposure management (CTEM), and application security posture management (ASPM). The market is effectively unbundling CNAPP into separate tools targeting distinct personas: developers (ASPM), security operations (CDR), and vulnerability management (CTEM), rather than maintaining an all-in-one platform.
