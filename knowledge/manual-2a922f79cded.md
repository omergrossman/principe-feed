Major cloud security vendors pivot away from CNAPP as market consolidates around Wiz

Check Point and Palo Alto Networks are repositioning their cloud security offerings, with Check Point partnering with Wiz and transitioning CloudGuard to focus on WAF, while Palo Alto shifts from Prisma Cloud to Cortex Cloud as a SOC-oriented platform. The moves reflect market acknowledgment that cloud posture scanning has become a commoditized space dominated by Wiz, prompting established vendors to pursue adjacent markets including runtime detection, vulnerability management, and AI security.
