Enterprise Defenses Show Strong Performance Against Simulated Attacks While Silent Attack Vectors Remain Vulnerable

Picus Labs' Blue Report 2026 analyzed over 338 million attack simulations across enterprise production environments, revealing that organizations have substantially improved their ability to detect and prevent noisy, conventional attacks. However, the data indicates that adversaries are increasingly succeeding through quieter attack methods that bypass traditional detection mechanisms, suggesting a gap between defensive tuning and emerging attack patterns.
