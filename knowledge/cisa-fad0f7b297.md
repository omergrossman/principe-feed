CISA Advisory: Critical Vulnerabilities in Grid Protection Alliance openPDC and openHistorian

Grid Protection Alliance has released patches for five critical vulnerabilities affecting openPDC (versions <2.9.482) and openHistorian (versions <2.8.585), including unsafe deserialization enabling remote code execution, missing authentication on data publisher interfaces, and use of hardcoded credentials. CVSS scores reach 9.8. Patches are available; however, Docker image variants remain unpatched and are not recommended for production use.
