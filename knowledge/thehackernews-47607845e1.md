NVIDIA Launches Open Secure AI Alliance with 37 Members

NVIDIA and 36 partner organizations have established the Open Secure AI Alliance to develop shared open-source technologies and practices for securing AI agents and software systems. The coalition includes major cloud, security, and enterprise software vendors such as Microsoft, Cisco, Cloudflare, CrowdStrike, and Palo Alto Networks, alongside open-source contributors like the Linux Foundation and Red Hat.
