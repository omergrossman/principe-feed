Miasma Malware Expands Supply Chain Campaign Across npm and Go Ecosystems

Researchers identified new malicious npm package releases targeting LeoPlatform and RStreams, along with GitHub Actions workflow abuse, as part of an ongoing supply chain campaign attributed to the Mini Shai-Hulud/Miasma/Hades malware family. The threat actor has extended its reach into the Go ecosystem, indicating a sustained effort to compromise multiple developer-focused platforms.
