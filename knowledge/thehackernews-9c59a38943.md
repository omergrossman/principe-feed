Synology NAS Devices Targeted in Ransomware Attacks Exploiting Patched DSM Flaw

Synology has identified ransomware campaigns exploiting a vulnerability in DiskStation Manager (DSM) software that was patched in December 2024. The attacks target NAS devices running unpatched DSM versions, with threat actors attempting data encryption and extortion. The vendor recommends customers apply available updates to mitigate exposure.
