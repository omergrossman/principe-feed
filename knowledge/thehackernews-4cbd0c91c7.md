Trump Executive Order Establishes 2030-2031 Timeline for Federal Post-Quantum Cryptography Migration

An executive order signed on June 22 mandates that U.S. federal agencies transition high-value assets and critical systems to post-quantum cryptographic standards by specified deadlines: key establishment by end of 2030 and digital signatures by end of 2031. National security systems operate under separate migration parameters. The directive reflects ongoing concerns about quantum computing threats to current encryption infrastructure.
