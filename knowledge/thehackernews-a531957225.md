Dysphoria IoT Botnet Adopts Blockchain C2 Infrastructure Post-Disruption

Dysphoria IoT botnet has evolved its command-and-control architecture to incorporate blockchain-based naming services and victim device relays following law enforcement action against JackSkid infrastructure in March. The architectural changes are designed to increase resilience against disruption and takedown operations. CNCERT and XLab researchers documented the modifications in their tracking efforts.
