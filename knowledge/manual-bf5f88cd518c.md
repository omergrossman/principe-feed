White House Executive Order on Post-Quantum Cryptography Transition

The U.S. Presidential Executive Order issued June 22, 2026, mandates a coordinated federal migration to NIST-approved post-quantum cryptography (PQC) standards to counter threats from future quantum computing capabilities and current harvest-now-decrypt-later attacks. Federal agencies must designate PQC migration leads within 30 days and transition high-value assets and high-impact systems to PQC-based key establishment by December 31, 2030, and digital signatures by December 31, 2031. OMB, NIST, NSA, and CISA are tasked with providing technical guidance, with NIST conducting a PQC pilot by end of 2027. Sector Risk Management Agencies must assist critical infrastructure operators in developing migration plans, while the State Department coordinates international adoption of NIST PQC standards. The order also requires development of cryptographic bills of materials and cost optimization strategies across federal procurement.
