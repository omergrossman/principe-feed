Eight Out-of-Bounds Read Vulnerabilities in MZ Automation libiec61850

CISA disclosed eight out-of-bounds read vulnerabilities in MZ Automation GmbH libiec61850 versions prior to 1.6.2, affecting IEC 61850 GOOSE and MMS protocol handlers deployed in energy infrastructure worldwide. Exploitation via specially crafted Layer-2 multicast frames or MMS messages can trigger denial-of-service conditions by crashing subscriber and service processes. The vendor has released version 1.6.2 as mitigation.
