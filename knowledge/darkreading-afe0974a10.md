Venezuelan Cartel Cybercriminal Arrested for ATM Jackpotting Operations

A cybercriminal associated with Tren de Aragua, a Venezuelan criminal organization, was apprehended after developing malware used for ATM jackpotting attacks. The individual was the first cybercriminal to appear on the FBI's Most Wanted Fugitives list, highlighting law enforcement's focus on transnational cybercriminal networks that fund violent criminal enterprises.
