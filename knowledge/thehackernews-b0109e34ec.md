Three Teams Exploit Fully Patched Google Pixel 10 at Pwn2Own Ireland

Three research teams successfully demonstrated remote exploits against a fully patched Google Pixel 10 during the Pwn2Own Ireland competition in Cork. One team, Ikotas Labs, earned the contest's top prize of $300,000 for their exploit, securing overall victory in the event. The contest requires all targets to be fully patched and provides researchers financial incentive while ensuring vulnerabilities are disclosed to vendors.
