PHP2Shell Exploit Attempts Target Millions; Mirai Botnet Campaign Largely Unsuccessful

Threat actors have launched widespread exploitation attempts against PHP2Shell vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, CVSS 9.2) to recruit systems into a Mirai botnet variant called Potassium. Early indications suggest these attacks have been largely unsuccessful, with exploitation proving more challenging than initially anticipated.
