China-Linked Group Exploited VMware ESX Zero-Day to Suppress Audit Logs

CISA added CVE-2024-41709, a VMware ESX vulnerability, to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog following disclosure that a China-nexus actor designated Warp Panda weaponized it against U.S. legal sector organizations. The flaw allows attackers to reduce visibility into system audit trails, facilitating post-compromise activity concealment.
