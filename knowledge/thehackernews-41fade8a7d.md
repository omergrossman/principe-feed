TuxBot v3 Evolution: IoT Botnet Framework Shows LLM-Assisted Development

Researchers identified TuxBot v3 Evolution, an IoT botnet framework that exhibits characteristics suggesting large language model assistance in its development, though the implementation contains notable flaws. The malware framework demonstrates how threat actors are experimenting with AI tools to accelerate botnet creation, while safety mechanisms in LLMs appear to have limited practical impact on final outputs.
