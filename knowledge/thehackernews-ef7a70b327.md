Malicious LiteLLM PyPI Packages Exposed 2,100+ Organizations to Credential Theft

Two compromised LiteLLM packages were published to PyPI in March 2024 for approximately 40 minutes, containing code designed to extract cloud credentials, SSH keys, Kubernetes tokens, and database passwords from affected systems. CloudSEK's analysis of 434,000 captured files indicates potential exposure across 2,100+ organizations, resulting from the supply chain compromise linked to the Trivy project breach.
