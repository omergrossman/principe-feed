XZ Utils Vulnerability Affecting B&R Industrial Automation Products

B&R Industrial Automation has released patches for multiple product lines vulnerable to CVE-2025-31115, a race condition in XZ Utils versions 5.3.3alpha through 5.8.0 that can cause application crashes and memory corruption. The vulnerability, with a CVSS score of 7.5, is remotely exploitable and affects manufacturing control systems globally; updated versions are now available for all affected product lines.
