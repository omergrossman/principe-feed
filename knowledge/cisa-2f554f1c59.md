CISA Alerts Water Sector to PLC Compromise Campaign

CISA is tracking coordinated attacks against programmable logic controllers in water and wastewater systems, where threat actors modify passwords and reconfigure network settings to disable operator access, resulting in operational disruptions and boil water notices. The campaign affects organizations of all sizes and exploits internet-exposed OT devices, including undocumented cellular modems. CISA recommends immediate disconnection of exposed PLCs from the internet, implementation of VPN-based remote access controls, credential management, and IP allowlisting.
