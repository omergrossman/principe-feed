ABB KNX Update Tool Firmware Integrity Vulnerability

ABB disclosed a firmware integrity validation flaw (CVE-2026-12705) affecting KNX Update Tool versions up to 2.0.175, with a CVSS score of 6.4. The vulnerability is limited to legacy KNX devices predating the KNX Secure standard and requires physical bus access to exploit. ABB will not issue a software patch due to architectural constraints in the legacy KNX protocol and recommends avoiding legacy devices for sensitive functions such as access control.
