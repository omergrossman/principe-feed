Multiple Critical Vulnerabilities in Naxclow IoT Platform

CISA disclosed five vulnerabilities affecting all versions of Naxclow's IoT devices including smart doorbells, home hubs, and cameras. Flaws include hardcoded cryptographic keys, missing authorization checks, non-rotating credentials, and predictable device identifiers that collectively enable device takeover, credential harvesting, and communication interception. Naxclow did not respond to CISA coordination attempts; affected users should contact the vendor directly.
