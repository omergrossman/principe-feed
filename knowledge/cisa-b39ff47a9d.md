Johnson Controls EasyIO Neo Series Controllers Information Disclosure Vulnerability

CISA disclosed a vulnerability in Johnson Controls EasyIO Neo Series EC and CW Controllers that could permit attackers to access sensitive information for conducting follow-on attacks. Affected versions include EC V3.3b63/b62 and CW V3.3b25/b24; fixes are available in EC V3.3b64 and CW V3.3b26. Interim mitigations include physical access controls, network monitoring, and debug interface hardening pending patch deployment.
