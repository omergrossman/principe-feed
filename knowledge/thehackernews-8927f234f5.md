CISA Adds GitLab RCE and Cisco Firewall Flaws to Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog

CISA has catalogued two vulnerabilities as actively exploited: a critical remote code execution flaw in GitLab (CVE-2026-97229, CVSS 10.0) resulting from integer overflow, and a denial-of-service vulnerability in Cisco firewall products. Inclusion in the KEV catalog signals widespread exploitation and compliance requirements for federal agencies and critical infrastructure operators.
