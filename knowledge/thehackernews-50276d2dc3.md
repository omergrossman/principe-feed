ValleyRAT Backdoor Distributed via Signed Adware with AV Exclusions

Silver Fox threat actors have been distributing ValleyRAT backdoor malware disguised as QN Wallpaper, a legitimate Chinese adware application. The malware leverages code signing and operates under trusted processes to evade detection by users who manually exclude the adware from antivirus scanning. Kaspersky identified the campaign and noted the attackers' strategy of abusing legitimate software trust mechanisms.
