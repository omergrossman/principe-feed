8 Moats Test: Framework for Competitive Durability Assessment

A strategic framework identifies eight sources of competitive advantage that determine whether a business can sustain its market position. The eight moats include proprietary data accumulation, operational workflow embedding, regulatory barriers, proprietary distribution channels, ecosystem dependencies, network effects, physical infrastructure assets, and scale-driven cost advantages. The assessment model rates competitive durability based on moat count: companies with four or more moats are considered durable, those with two to three face sustainability questions, and those with one or fewer require rapid capability expansion or face material competitive risk. This framework serves as a practical tool for evaluating whether technology and business model investments create defensible positions.
