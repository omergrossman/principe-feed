Hitachi Energy RTU500 Multiple Vulnerabilities in CMU Firmware

CISA disclosed multiple vulnerabilities affecting Hitachi Energy RTU500 series CMU firmware versions 12.7.1 through 13.8.1, including null pointer dereferences and integer overflow issues in PKCS#12 processing and libexpat XML parsing. Exploitation primarily causes denial of service, with potential secondary impacts on confidentiality and integrity when IEC 61850 functionality or PKI client features are configured. Hitachi Energy recommends updating to CMU firmware 13.8.2 or 13.7.9.
