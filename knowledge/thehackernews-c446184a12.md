Amazon Attributes npm Package Hijacks to North Korea's Sapphire Sleet

Amazon has attributed the September 2025 compromise of widely-used npm packages debug and chalk to North Korea's Sapphire Sleet threat actor. The incident, initially assessed as financially motivated, involved phishing a maintainer via a lookalike domain and injecting wallet-draining code into at least 18 packages collectively downloaded over 2 billion times weekly. The attribution provides clarity on a supply chain attack that remained publicly documented for ten months without definitive actor attribution.
