Attackers Exploit PaperCut Flaws to Steal Credentials From Schools and Universities

Threat actors are exploiting recently disclosed PaperCut vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-81578 and CVE-2026-82078) combining authentication bypass with remote code execution to target educational institutions. Arctic Wolf researchers observed attackers leveraging these flaws to execute commands, perform reconnaissance, and harvest credentials from affected organizations in the U.S. and Europe.
