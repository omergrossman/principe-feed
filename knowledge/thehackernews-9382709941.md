SilkParasite Espionage Campaign Deploys Five Undocumented RATs Against Central Asian Governments

A previously unreported espionage operation named SilkParasite has targeted government entities in Central Asia using seven RAT families, five of which are newly documented. The campaign, identified in late 2025, employs tools including DriveSilkRAT, CookiETagRAT, NomadRAT, GoginRAT, and NodeEdgeRAT to maintain persistent access to victim networks.
