The Gentlemen Ransomware Operation Claims 478 Victims with Worm-like Propagation Capability

The Gentlemen threat group, initially operating as a ransomware affiliate leveraging LockBit, Qilin, and Medusa RaaS platforms, has evolved into an independent operation claiming 478 victims. The group employs double extortion tactics and has developed capabilities enabling worm-like spread across compromised networks.
