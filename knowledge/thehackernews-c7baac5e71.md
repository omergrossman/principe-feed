Critical SonicWall SMA 1000 Vulnerability Actively Exploited Post-Disclosure

A critical pre-authenticated SSRF vulnerability (CVE-2026-102255, CVSS 10.0) in SonicWall Secure Mobile Access 1000 appliances has been targeted in at least 135 exploitation attempts following public disclosure. The flaw enables remote attackers to direct affected appliances to issue unauthorized requests, creating immediate risk for organizations operating vulnerable infrastructure.
