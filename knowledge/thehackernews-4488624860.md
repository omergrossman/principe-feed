Threat Actors Probe Gitea Docker Vulnerability CVE-2026-20896 Post-Disclosure

Sysdig observed threat actors attempting to exploit CVE-2026-20896, a critical flaw in Gitea Docker images disclosed 13 days prior. The vulnerability allows unauthenticated attackers to bypass authentication by spoofing the X-WEBAUTH-USER header, with a CVSS score of 9.8.
