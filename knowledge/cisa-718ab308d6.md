CISA Adds Two Known Exploited Vulnerabilities to KEV Catalog

CISA has cataloged two vulnerabilities with evidence of active exploitation: a command injection flaw in BerriAI LiteLLM and an authentication bypass in Check Point Security Gateway. Federal agencies are required to remediate KEV Catalog entries under BOD 22-01, though CISA recommends all organizations prioritize patching these vulnerabilities as part of routine vulnerability management.
