Tech Firms Dismantle Botnet Leveraging 1.3M Compromised TV Boxes

Google and industry partners dismantled a botnet comprising over 1.3 million Android TV set-top boxes pre-installed with malicious software that hijacked user internet connections for proxy services and ad fraud schemes. The takedown is expected to disrupt the botnet's command infrastructure across an estimated 10 million additional compromised IoT devices, including healthcare equipment and entertainment systems running Android-based platforms.
