U.S. Army Soldier Sentenced to 70 Months for Telecommunications Hacking and Extortion

A U.S. Army soldier pleaded guilty to unauthorized access of telecommunications infrastructure and theft of call and text metadata affecting over 100 million AT&T customers during 2024. The defendant was sentenced to 70 months in federal prison and ordered to pay approximately $300,000 in restitution. The case underscores insider threat risks within critical infrastructure operators.
