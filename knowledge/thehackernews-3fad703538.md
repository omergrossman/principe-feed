OpenAI Releases GPT-5.6 Preview Models with Enhanced Security Controls

OpenAI introduced three variants of GPT-5.6 (Sol, Terra, and Luna) in limited preview to select organizations, with Sol representing the flagship capability tier. The release incorporates enhanced cybersecurity safeguards and follows coordination with U.S. government stakeholders. The tiered approach allows organizations to select models optimized for capability, efficiency, or cost depending on use case requirements.
