Slopsquatting, Phantom Domains, and HalluSquatting Share Common Attack Mechanism

Three seemingly distinct attack variants—slopsquatting, phantom squatting, and HalluSquatting—exploit a unified late-binding vulnerability where AI coding agents trust non-existent package names, repositories, or domains generated by language models. Mitigation strategies include pre-fetch verification and governed dependency management to prevent malicious code injection into software supply chains.
