Amadey and StealC Malware Network Disrupted, 27M Stolen Credentials Recovered

Law enforcement and private sector partners including Bitdefender, Bitsight, ESET, and Microsoft have dismantled criminal infrastructure associated with Amadey and StealC malware variants. The operation recovered approximately 27 million stolen credentials and targeted the distribution chains used to facilitate ransomware deployments, financial fraud, and attacks against critical infrastructure.
