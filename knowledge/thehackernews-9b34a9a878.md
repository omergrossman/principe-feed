WSO2 and Adobe Commerce Vulnerabilities Added to CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog

CISA has catalogued two critical vulnerabilities affecting WSO2 API Control Plane and Adobe Commerce as actively exploited in the wild. CVE-2026-5430, a path traversal flaw in WSO2 with a CVSS score of 9.8, is among those added to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog based on confirmed exploitation evidence.
