Government Linux Infrastructure Targeted in Espionage Campaign with Custom Malware

Palo Alto Networks Unit 42 identified a cyber espionage campaign between October 2025 and April 2026 targeting Linux systems across government and state-affiliated entities. The operation, tracked as CL-UNK-1211, deployed custom malware including SysGoRAT alongside potentially AI-generated tooling to establish persistence and exfiltrate data.
