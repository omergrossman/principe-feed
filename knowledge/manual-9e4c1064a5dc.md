CyberRatings.org and NSS Labs Release 2025 Enterprise Firewall Evaluation Results

NSS Labs evaluated seven enterprise firewalls using encrypted workloads and over 26,000 attack samples, revealing significant performance variance with security effectiveness ranging from 46.37% to 99.59%. The assessment identified critical evasion vulnerabilities in widely deployed products, with only three firewalls earning a Recommended rating, and highlighted that encrypted traffic inspection remains a key differentiator among vendors.
