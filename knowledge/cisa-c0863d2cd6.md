InterSystems IRIS for Health TLS/SSL Encryption Disabled by Default

CISA disclosed a vulnerability in InterSystems IRIS for Health versions 2025.3.0.226, 2026.1.0.235, and 2026.2.0.221 where TLS/SSL encryption is disabled by default, allowing attackers to intercept transmitted data and potentially obtain authentication credentials. The vulnerability carries a CVSS v3.1 score of 8.1 and a CVSS v4.0 score of 9.2. InterSystems recommends configuring the IRIS superserver to require mandatory TLS encryption rather than leaving it optional or disabled.
