Coldcard Hardware Wallet Firmware Flaw Associated with $70M Bitcoin Theft

An attacker exploited a firmware vulnerability in Coldcard hardware wallets to drain approximately 1,082 BTC (~$70.2M) from 1,196 addresses within 41 minutes on July 30. Galaxy Research attributed the incident to a March 2021 integration error that caused seed generation to use a deterministic software PRNG instead of proper cryptographic randomness, potentially compromising key derivation for affected devices.
