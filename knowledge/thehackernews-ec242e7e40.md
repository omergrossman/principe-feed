Anthropic Disrupts Industrial-Scale Claude Distillation Attacks from Chinese AI Labs

Anthropic identified and disrupted unauthorized distillation attacks against Claude originating from seven Chinese AI laboratories, including Alibaba, DeepSeek, and Moonshot. The company detected attempts to extract model capabilities at scale through knowledge distillation, a technique that transfers learned patterns from one model to another. Anthropic took action to prevent further unauthorized access and model replication.
