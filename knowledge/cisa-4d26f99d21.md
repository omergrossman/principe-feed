Rockwell Automation Communication Modules Vulnerable to Denial-of-Service via Packet Validation Flaw

CISA disclosed a vulnerability affecting Rockwell Automation 1756-EN2, 1756-EN3, and 1756-ENBT communication modules that allows network-based attackers to disrupt device connections through improper validation of CIP Implicit Connection packets. Affected versions include EN3 and EN2 up to V12.001, and ENBT V6.006; patches are available for EN2 and EN3, though EN3 is discontinued. No active exploitation has been reported.
