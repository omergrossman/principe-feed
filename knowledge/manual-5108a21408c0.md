AI-SPM buyer's guide: 14 tools to secure your AI infrastructure

Enterprise adoption of AI has driven demand for AI security posture management (AI-SPM) tools, a category focused on monitoring and securing AI models, data pipelines, and services. The market spans three organizational maturity stages—AI-assisted, AI-augmented, and AI-native—each requiring distinct security approaches. Microsoft's 2026 Cyber Pulse report found that while 80% of Fortune 500 companies deploy active AI agents, only 10% have clear governance strategies; the average enterprise runs 37 agents with over half lacking security oversight or logging. Major security vendors including Palo Alto Networks (which acquired Protect.ai), SentinelOne (Prompt.Security), Varonis (Cyral, SlashNext), and others have incorporated AI-SPM capabilities into broader portfolios. The guide reviews 14 leading vendors and notes that AI-SPM tools typically employ agentless architectures to protect cloud-based models while preventing data exfiltration and configuration drift, with integrations spanning cloud, data, and application security platforms.
