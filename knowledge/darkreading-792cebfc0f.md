IBM Invests $5B in Project Lightwell to Address AI-Identified Open-Source Vulnerabilities

IBM and Red Hat are dedicating 20,000 engineers to Project Lightwell, a new service aimed at securing the open-source software supply chain. The initiative was prompted by Anthropic's Mythos research, which used AI to identify bugs in open-source components, sparking industry discussion on vulnerability remediation and supply chain resilience.
