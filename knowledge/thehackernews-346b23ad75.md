CISA Adds Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN Manager Auth Bypass to KEV Catalog

CISA has catalogued a critical authentication bypass vulnerability (CVE-2026-76504, CVSS 9.8) in Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN Manager as actively exploited in the wild. The flaw permits unauthenticated remote access to affected systems, prompting expedited tracking within the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog.
