CISA Advisory: Multiple Critical Vulnerabilities in Ebyte NA111-M Gateway

CISA has disclosed thirteen vulnerabilities affecting Ebyte NA111-M network gateway firmware version 9013-2-17, with CVSS scores ranging from 7.5 to 9.8. The flaws span missing authentication, token mishandling, CSRF weaknesses, and insufficient access controls that could permit remote compromise of device functionality. Ebyte acknowledged the issues and stated patch development is underway, though the vendor has not provided status updates or timelines to CISA.
